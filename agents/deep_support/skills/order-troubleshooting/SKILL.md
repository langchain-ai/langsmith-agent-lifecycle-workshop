# Order troubleshooting

## Steps

1. Confirm the customer is verified. If not, ask them to verify before you look anything up.
2. Ask the `order-specialist` for the order's status, order date, ship date, and items.
3. Match the situation:
   - `Processing` for more than 3 days: apologize, say it is being prioritized, and offer cancellation (per the Escalation Policy).
   - `Shipped`: give the ship date and the expected delivery window from the Shipping Guide (ask the `product-policy-specialist`).
   - `Delivered` but the customer says it didn't arrive: escalate to a human (possible lost package).
   - Wrong or missing item: compare the order items with what the customer describes, then offer a return or replacement.
4. Never guess a tracking status the tools didn't return.

## Output

- Current status in one line.
- What happens next, and when.
- Any action you took or are escalating.
