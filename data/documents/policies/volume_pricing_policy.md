# Volume and Bulk Purchasing Policy

TechHub does not operate a published volume-discount, bulk-pricing, or wholesale program. This policy applies to all customers, including business and corporate accounts.

## Pricing

- All products are sold at the standard listed price shown on the product page, regardless of the quantity ordered.
- There are no quantity thresholds, tiered pricing tables, or bulk price breaks.
- There are no corporate, enterprise, reseller, or wholesale pricing tiers.
- Negotiated pricing agreements and custom quotes are not available.
- Published promotions and promo codes apply equally to all customers and cannot be combined into a bulk arrangement.

## Accounts and Payment Terms

- TechHub does not maintain a corporate-sales directory and does not assign dedicated account managers or procurement contacts.
- Standard payment terms apply to every order. Net 30, net 60, invoiced billing, and purchase-order billing are not offered.
- Accepted payment methods are the same for all orders (see the [Customer Support FAQ](support_faq.md)).

## Large Orders

- Large orders are placed through the standard checkout process and are subject to the stock quantity available for each product.
- Standard shipping options and timelines apply; there is no expedited bulk fulfillment path or freight program.

## Where to Direct Bulk and Corporate Enquiries

Customers with bulk, volume, or corporate purchasing questions should be directed to the standard support channels only:

- Phone: 1-800-555-TECH (Mon-Fri 8 AM - 8 PM ET, Sat-Sun 9 AM - 5 PM ET)
- Email: support@techhub.com (response within 24 hours)
- Live Chat: Available on our website during business hours

## Guidance for Support Agents

Do not quote discounts, estimate bulk lead times, or claim that a bulk or corporate request has been escalated, forwarded, or assigned to a sales team. State that no documented volume-pricing program exists and share the contact channels above.
